### Maintainer notes

OpenClash is a native bar widget for controlling a user-configured remote
Mihomo/OpenClash instance. It stores the controller URL and secret only under
the user's Omarchy configuration directory, performs no installation hooks or
privileged operations, and remains usable when the private controller is
offline.
